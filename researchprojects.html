Pulling atomic-scale particles using matter wave (Ongoing)

Journal Article: Quantum Mechanical Lateral Force on an Atom due to Matter Wave (Completed)

Journal Article:

Presentation:

This article demonstrates an asymmetric setup that engenders reversible lateral force on an atom due to the interaction energy of the matter wave in the presence of a metal surface. By solving the time-independent Schrödinger equation and using the solution, quantum mechanical stress tensor formalism is applied to compute the force acting on the particle.

Undergraduate Junior Design Project: Self-ordering Kiosk System using Arduino (Completed)

Project Report:

A study on the latest kiosk technology is done, and the possible features which can be included on budget are identified and implemented on this project. Finally, the paper shows the use of Arduino, NodeMCU, Thin-Film Transistor(TFT) display, and Digital Logic Design (DLD) components.
